How the calculation works

Risk in EUR = balance × risk %.
Units = (risk in EUR × rate) ÷ (stop in pips × pip size), where the pip size is 0.0001 for most pairs and 0.01 when the quote currency is JPY.
Lots = units ÷ lot size. The lot size is rounded down to 0.01 so you never risk more than planned.

Assumes a standard forex pair with 4 decimals (or 2 for JPY pairs). It does not cover indices, metals, crypto or commissions, and it does not account for spread or slippage. Your broker may use different contract sizes or minimum lots.
