Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and how you can control it. The site offers a free position size calculator and an optional email sign-up.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The website is operated by dscom. Contact: privacy@danielsobral.com.

2. The calculator

The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter (balance, risk, stop loss, rates) are not sent to us or to any third party and are not stored.

3. Email sign-up

If you subscribe, we collect:

  • your email address;
  • your consent to receive emails, recorded when you tick the box;
  • the page you signed up from.

We use this data only to send you occasional emails about our tools and new releases. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

Legal basis (EU and UK users): your consent, which you give by ticking the box. You can withdraw it at any time.

4. Who processes the data

Sign-up submissions are handled by Formspree, a third-party form service. Formspree stores the submission and forwards it to us. Its own privacy policy applies to that processing. Formspree may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@danielsobral.com. We will reply within one month.

7. Unsubscribing

Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe. You can also ask us by email at any time.

8. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not use analytics tools. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under its own policy.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
